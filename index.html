🧬 Human Evolution Lab

🦴

Fossils

Fossils preserve evidence of ancient life. Bones, teeth and footprints help researchers investigate the past.

🧬

DNA & Genetics

DNA carries inherited biological information. Comparing genomes helps scientists study evolutionary relationships.

🌍

Human Origins

Homo sapiens evolved in Africa and later spread around the world.

Evolution Timeline

Early hominins: Several species show adaptations for upright walking.

Genus Homo: Different human relatives used tools and adapted to changing environments.

Homo sapiens: Our species arose in Africa roughly 300,000 years ago, based on current evidence.

🦕 Prehistoric World

Triassic

The first dinosaurs appeared over 230 million years ago.

Jurassic

Dinosaurs diversified, including many large sauropods and theropods.

Cretaceous

Many non-bird dinosaurs disappeared about 66 million years ago; birds survived as dinosaur descendants.

🏛️ Ancient Civilizations

Ancient Egypt

The Nile supported farming, cities, monumental architecture and a long-lasting civilization.

Indus Civilization

Harappa and Mohenjo-daro are known for planned streets and sophisticated drainage.

Mesopotamia

Early cities between the Tigris and Euphrates developed complex administration and writing.
